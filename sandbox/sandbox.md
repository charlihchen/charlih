# Sandbox

## Essay vs. Thesis

Differences Between an Essay and a Thesis
Definition and Purpose
| Aspect	| Essay	| Thesis |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Length | Typically 5-20 pages	| Usually 30-400 pages, depending on the degree |
| Purpose	| Assigned for a course to explore a topic| Required for a degree, presenting a comprehensive analysis |
| Complexity | Less complex, focused on a single argument	| More complex, integrating multiple arguments and research |

Structure

    * Essay:
        - Introduction
        - Body paragraphs supporting the main argument
        - Conclusion summarizing the findings

    * Thesis:
        - Introduction with a clear thesis statement
        - Multiple chapters or sections, each addressing different aspects of the research question
        - Comprehensive conclusion synthesizing the overall findings

Argumentation

    * Essay:
        - Presents a single argument or perspective on a topic.
        - Aims to persuade or inform based on evidence.

    * Thesis:
        - Combines various arguments and analyses.
        - Requires extensive research and critical analysis to support a central claim.

Conclusion

Essays and theses serve different academic purposes. Essays are shorter and focus on specific arguments, while theses are longer, more complex documents that synthesize multiple essays into a comprehensive analysis required for degree completion.
